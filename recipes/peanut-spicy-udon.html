Peanut Spicy Udon

Creamy peanut udon noodles – spicy, nutty and packed with flavour. Everything comes together in one pan.

Time
20 min
Servings
2
Effort
Easy
Share: WhatsApp

Ingredients

For 2 servings

  • 250 g Udon noodles
  • 3 tbsp Peanut butter
  • 1 Small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 Garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 1 tsp Chilli flakes
  • 2 Scallions, sliced
  • 1 handful Fresh coriander, chopped
  • 1 tbsp Sesame seeds

Hosting a dinner?

This dish fits the murder mystery Radio Silence on Ares.

6–8 players · approx. 3 hrs · instant PDF

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the udon noodles according to the package instructions and keep a cup of the cooking water.

  2. 2

    Fry the onion and garlic in the pan until soft, then add the scallions, chilli flakes and sesame seeds.

  3. 3

    Stir in the peanut butter and the soy sauce, then loosen the sauce with a splash of the noodle water until creamy.

  4. 4

    Add the noodles and toss until every strand is coated.

  5. 5

    Fold in the fresh coriander and serve straight from the pan.
