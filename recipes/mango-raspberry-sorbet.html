Mango Raspberry Sorbet

Refreshing, fruity and naturally sweet – four ingredients and about a minute of work.

Time
5 min
Servings
2
Effort
Easy
Share: WhatsApp

Ingredients

For 2 servings

  • 250 g Frozen mango
  • 150 g Frozen raspberries
  • 15 g Lemon juice
  • 20 g Honey
