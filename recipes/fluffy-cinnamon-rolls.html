Fluffy Cinnamon Rolls

Perfect for autumn – soft, fluffy, filled with cinnamon and finished with cream cheese frosting while still warm.

Time
50 min + 90 min
Servings
10
Effort
Medium
Share: WhatsApp

Ingredients

Makes 10 rolls

Dough

  • 270 ml Milk
  • 60 g Butter
  • 500 g All-purpose flour
  • 50 g Sugar
  • 8 g Vanilla sugar (1 packet)
  • 1 pinch Salt
  • 21 g Fresh yeast (or 7 g dry yeast)
  • A little butter for greasing and flour for rolling

Filling

  • 100 g Soft butter
  • 70 g Sugar
  • 2–3 tsp Cinnamon

Frosting

  • 70 g Cream cheese
  • 1 tbsp Soft butter
  • 1 tbsp Powdered sugar, heaped

Method

  1. 1

    Warm the milk and butter in a small pot until the butter has melted and the mixture is lukewarm (about 37 °C – it should feel neither warm nor cold on your finger). Crumble in the yeast and stir until it has dissolved.

  2. 2

    Mix the flour, sugar, vanilla sugar and salt in a large bowl. Add the milk mixture and knead for 5–8 minutes, by hand or with the dough hook, until the dough is smooth, soft and no longer sticky.

  3. 3

    Cover the bowl and let the dough rise in a warm place for about 1 hour, until it has doubled in size.

  4. 4

    Roll out the dough on a lightly floured surface into a rectangle of about 40 × 30 cm.

  5. 5

    Mix the soft butter, sugar and cinnamon into a paste and spread it evenly over the dough, right to the edges.

  6. 6

    Roll up tightly from the long side and cut into 10 rolls with a sharp knife.

  7. 7

    Place the rolls cut side up in a greased baking dish (about 20 × 30 cm), cover and let them rise for another 30 minutes. Meanwhile, heat the oven to 180 °C (350 °F) conventional, or 160 °C fan.

  8. 8

    Bake for 20–25 minutes, until golden brown.

  9. 9

    Beat the cream cheese, butter and powdered sugar until creamy and spread over the warm rolls. They are best on the day they are baked.
