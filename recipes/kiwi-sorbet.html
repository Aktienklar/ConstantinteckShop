Kiwi Sorbet

The perfect refreshing summer treat – frozen kiwis and passionfruit, blended in five steps.

Time
5 min
Servings
2
Effort
Easy
Share: WhatsApp

Ingredients

For 2 servings

  • 4 Frozen kiwis
  • 2 Passionfruits
  • 2–3 tbsp Sugar
  • 1 tbsp Lemon juice

Hosting a dinner?

This dish fits the murder mystery Closing Bell on the 24th Floor.

6–7 players · approx. 3 hrs · instant PDF

Method

  1. 1

    Add the frozen kiwis to a blender.

  2. 2

    Scoop in the passionfruit pulp.

  3. 3

    Add the sugar and lemon juice.

  4. 4

    Blend until smooth and creamy.

  5. 5

    Serve immediately, or freeze for 30 minutes for extra creaminess.
