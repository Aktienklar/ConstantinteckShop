Steak Loaded Sweet Potato

A baked sweet potato loaded with tomato salad, lemon herb yogurt, avocado and juicy sliced steak.

Time
60 min
Servings
2
Effort
Medium

Ingredients

For 2 servings

For the steak

  • 2 Steaks of your choice, about 200 g each
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper

For the sweet potato

  • 2 Large sweet potatoes
  • 1 Avocado
  • 2 Spring onions, sliced

For the lemon herb yogurt sauce

  • 150 g Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp Fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp Fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 tbsp Fresh chives, chopped
  • Salt and black pepper

For the tomato & red onion salad

  • 200 g Cherry tomatoes
  • ½ Red onion
  • 1 tbsp Extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp Balsamic vinegar
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. 1

    Heat the oven to 200 °C (400 °F). Wash the sweet potatoes, prick them all over with a fork and bake them on a lined tray for 45–60 minutes, until a knife slides in without resistance.

  2. 2

    Take the steaks out of the fridge about 30 minutes before cooking and pat them dry with kitchen paper.

  3. 3

    For the salad, quarter the cherry tomatoes and finely dice the red onion. Mix with the olive oil and balsamic vinegar and season with salt and pepper.

  4. 4

    For the sauce, stir the Greek yogurt with the lemon juice, parsley and chives and season with salt and pepper.

  5. 5

    Heat a pan over high heat until it is very hot. Rub the steaks with olive oil, season with salt and sear for 2–3 minutes per side for medium-rare (for a steak about 2.5 cm thick). Let them rest on a board for 5 minutes, season with pepper and slice thinly against the grain.

  6. 6

    Cut the sweet potatoes open lengthwise, fluff up the inside with a fork and season with a pinch of salt.

  7. 7

    Load them with the tomato and red onion salad, the lemon herb yogurt sauce and sliced avocado, top with the steak and finish with fresh spring onions. Enjoy!
