Crunchy Chicken Burger

Buttermilk-marinated chicken breast in a spicy, extra-crunchy coating, stacked in a soft brioche bun with crisp lettuce and a garlicky burger sauce.

Time
35 min + 30 min
Servings
4
Effort
Medium
Share: WhatsApp

Ingredients

For 4 burgers

For the chicken

  • 600 g Chicken breast (4 small fillets)

For the marinade

  • 250 ml Buttermilk
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 1 tsp Sweet paprika powder
  • ½ tsp Chilli powder

For the coating

  • 150 g Plain flour
  • 50 g Potato starch
  • 2 tsp Sweet paprika powder
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 1 tsp Chilli powder
  • Neutral oil for frying, e.g. sunflower (about 1 l)

For the burger sauce

  • 4 tbsp Mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp Ketchup
  • 1 Small garlic clove, finely grated
  • ½ tsp Sweet paprika powder
  • 1 pinch Chilli powder
  • 1 pinch Salt

To assemble

  • 4 Brioche burger buns
  • 4–8 Leaves of green lettuce, e.g. lollo bionda

Method

  1. 1

    Pat the chicken fillets dry and flatten them gently to an even thickness of about 1.5 cm, so they cook through evenly.

  2. 2

    For the marinade, stir the salt, paprika and chilli into the buttermilk. Turn the chicken in it, cover and leave it in the fridge for at least 30 minutes – overnight is even better.

  3. 3

    For the sauce, stir the mayonnaise, ketchup, garlic, paprika, chilli and salt together and keep it in the fridge until serving.

  4. 4

    For the coating, mix the flour, potato starch, paprika, salt and chilli in a wide dish. Drizzle in 2–3 tbsp of the marinade and rub it in with your fingers until small crumbs form – they give the extra crunch.

  5. 5

    Lift each fillet out of the buttermilk, let it drip briefly and press it firmly into the coating until it is thickly covered all over.

  6. 6

    Heat the oil in a deep pot to 170–175 °C. Fry the chicken two pieces at a time for 5–6 minutes, turning once, until deep golden and cooked through (74 °C in the centre). Drain on a rack.

  7. 7

    Cut the buns open and toast them cut side down in a dry pan for a minute until golden.

  8. 8

    Spread sauce on both halves of each bun. Lay lettuce on the bottom half, add the crunchy chicken, put the lid on and serve straight away.
