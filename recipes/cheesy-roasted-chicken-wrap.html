Cheesy Roasted Chicken Wrap

Cheesy, juicy and packed with flavour – chicken and peppers roast on one tray, then go into a tortilla with melted cheese.

Time
35 min
Servings
2
Effort
Easy

Video coming soon

Share: WhatsApp

Ingredients

For 2 servings

  • 2 Chicken breasts
  • 1 Red bell pepper
  • 1 Green bell pepper
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 3 Garlic cloves, minced (one of them for the sauce)
  • 1 tsp Paprika powder, plus a pinch for the sauce
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 200 g Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 4 Tortilla wraps
  • 100 g Cheese, grated

Method

  1. 1

    Chop the chicken and peppers, add them to a baking dish and mix with the olive oil, garlic, paprika, salt and pepper.

  2. 2

    Roast at 200 °C (400 °F) for 20–25 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the veggies are nicely roasted.

  3. 3

    Meanwhile stir the Greek yogurt with the lemon juice, garlic, salt, pepper and a pinch of paprika into a smooth sauce.

  4. 4

    Add cheese to a tortilla and heat until perfectly melted.

  5. 5

    Top with the roasted chicken and veggies and the yogurt sauce, wrap it up and enjoy that cheese pull.
