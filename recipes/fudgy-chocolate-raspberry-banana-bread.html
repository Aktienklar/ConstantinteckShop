Fudgy Chocolate Raspberry Banana Bread

Moist, chocolatey and bursting with juicy raspberries – soft, fudgy and mixed in a single bowl.

Time
65 min
Servings
10
Effort
Easy

Video coming soon

Share: WhatsApp

Ingredients

For 10 servings

  • 3 (250 g) Ripe bananas
  • 2 Eggs
  • 200 g Whole spelt flour
  • 100 ml Plant-based milk
  • ½ tsp Baking powder
  • Pinch of salt
  • 150 g Fresh raspberries
  • 80–100 g Dark chocolate chips

Method

  1. 1

    Mash the bananas and whisk in the eggs and plant-based milk.

  2. 2

    Stir in the flour, baking powder and salt.

  3. 3

    Fold in the raspberries and chocolate chips.

  4. 4

    Transfer to a lined loaf pan and top with extra chocolate and raspberries.

  5. 5

    Bake at 180 °C (350 °F) for 45–55 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out mostly clean.
