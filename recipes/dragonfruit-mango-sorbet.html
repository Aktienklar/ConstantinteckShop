Dragonfruit Mango Sorbet

Refreshing, tropical and naturally sweet – four frozen ingredients straight from the blender.

Time
5 min
Servings
2
Effort
Easy
Share: WhatsApp

Ingredients

For 2 servings

  • 250 g Frozen dragonfruit
  • 150 g Frozen mango
  • 15 g Lemon juice
  • 20 g Honey

Method

  1. 1

    Blend everything until smooth and creamy.

  2. 2

    Enjoy immediately as a soft sorbet, or freeze for 30–60 minutes for a firmer texture.
