Thick Mango Chia Pudding

Five ingredients and one ripe mango – blended, topped with chia and left to set in the fridge.

Time
10 min + 4 h
Servings
2
Effort
Easy

Video coming soon

Share: WhatsApp

Ingredients

For 2 servings

  • 1 Ripe mango
  • 150 ml Coconut milk
  • 1 tsp Honey
  • 100 g Yogurt
  • 4 tbsp Chia seeds

Method

  1. 1

    Blend the mango, coconut milk, honey and yogurt until smooth.

  2. 2

    Pour the mixture into a bowl and add the chia seeds on top.

  3. 3

    Let it chill in the fridge for 4 hours, then serve.
