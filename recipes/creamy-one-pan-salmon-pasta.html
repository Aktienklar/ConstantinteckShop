Creamy One-Pan Salmon Pasta

Everything bakes in one dish – the feta melts into a creamy sauce and the salmon flakes right into it.

Time
35 min
Servings
2
Effort
Easy
Share: WhatsApp

Ingredients

For 2 servings

  • 250 g Pasta
  • 250 g Salmon fillet
  • 200 g Feta cheese
  • 200 g Cherry tomatoes
  • 2 Garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Italian herbs
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. 1

    Add the salmon, feta, cherry tomatoes, garlic, olive oil and Italian herbs to a baking dish.

  2. 2

    Bake at 200 °C (400 °F) for 20–25 minutes, until the salmon is cooked through.

  3. 3

    Meanwhile, cook the pasta according to the package instructions.

  4. 4

    Remove the baking dish from the oven and mix everything together until the feta creates a creamy sauce and the salmon flakes apart.

  5. 5

    Add the cooked pasta, toss until everything is well coated, and serve immediately.
