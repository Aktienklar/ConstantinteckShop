Who's at the table

6 characters – everyone plays one. Tap a name for more, no spoilers.

  • Margit HaugenDirector of the Fjell Mountain Hotel

    Margit has run the hotel since 1912 and knows every creaking floorboard in it. Tonight she is giving the farewell dinner for the last guests; as of tomorrow she no longer has a position.

  • Eirik Brekkethe hotel's mountain guide and ski instructor

    For eight winters Eirik has led the guests through the high mountains and kept charge of the ski room. He says little, and in any weather he can tell when it is about to turn.

  • Liv Mørkthe Consul's daughter

    Liv grew up in the hotel and has kept house for her father since her mother died. She learned of the hotel's sale from the newspaper.

  • Doctor S. Aasa doctor from Bergen, regular guest

    Doctor Aas has a practice in Bergen and has come to Fjell for two weeks every winter for many years, always staying in Room 7. In the house Aas is thought of as quiet, helpful and a good listener.

  • Jo Viktakes photographs for an illustrated magazine from Oslo

    Jo Vik is here to photograph the hotel's last evening for an illustrated magazine and has set up a darkroom in the old linen room. It is Jo's first time at Fjell, and Jo looks at everything very closely.

  • Nils Ødegaardstationmaster of the Fjell railway stop

    For twenty years Nils has kept the little stop below the hotel, with its telegraph and its signal lamps. He likes to tell how things used to be on the line. Tonight the storm has left him stranded at the hotel.
