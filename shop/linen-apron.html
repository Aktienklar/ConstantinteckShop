Details

Exactly the apron I wear in almost every video. Waffle piqué, cut and sewn by hand here in Germany – not a factory run, which is why the seams are worth a second look and why there are never many in stock at once. The waffle weave is soft on the skin, absorbent enough to dry your hands on, and hard-wearing enough to outlast the recipes on this site. Adjustable neck strap and a large front pocket for a dough scraper, your phone or a kitchen towel. Unisex cut, one size, in three colours – Sand is the one from the videos, Mauve and Grey are the same fabric in a different mood. Machine washable at 40 °C – chocolate and tomato sauce stains build character.
